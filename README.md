# Steal-a-Noob-
Steal a Noob! is a funny stealing simulator. Steal eggs from Guardians like the Don't-Care Parent and Fat Gym Teacher, then escape to your Safe Zone! Plant eggs, wait for them to hatch into Noobs, and earn money per second. Upgrade your pen and Aura Treadmill to earn more money, collect rarer Noobs, and become rich!
